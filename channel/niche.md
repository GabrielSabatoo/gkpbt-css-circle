# Channel niche: aviation explainers

Decided 2026-11-10. Research from NexLev (channel videos + faceless outlier videos since 2026-05).

## Format
- 8–12 minute animated explainers answering one surprising "why" question about flying.
- Visuals: animated maps, flight routes on a globe, simple 2D diagrams (Remotion). No reused footage.
- Voice: British narrator (`voice/channel_voice.json`), calm documentary tone.
- Language: English (US/UK audience).

## What the data says
- Titles that win: a "Why…" question or a hidden thing ("The X No Airline Takes", "The Hidden Y Under Z"). Short, concrete, curiosity gap.
- Views are hit-driven: each channel has 1–3 videos with ~1M views and most others far below (Manicule's latest: 52K, 10K, 5.5K; CrewLife's latest: under 10K). Plan for volume and consistency; judge a video against the channel median, not the hits.
- Crash reconstructions are huge but news-driven and crowded (15+ channels covered the Florida Learjet crash within a week), involve real deaths, and demand speed and exact accuracy. Not a fit for an automated start.
- "Caught on camera" compilations use other people's footage: avoid (reused-content demonetization risk).

## Rules for every video
- Every fact in the script needs a source the Reviewer can check (manufacturer, regulator, airline, flight-tracking data).
- Original topic and angle: don't copy another channel's title or structure.
- Disclose AI voice in the description.
