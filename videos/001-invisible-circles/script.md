## Description (draft)
Every twin-engine airliner crossing an ocean has to stay inside a chain of invisible circles. This is the story of ETOPS ("Engines Turn Or Passengers Swim"): how a 1953 rule kept two-engine jets away from the oceans, how the circles grew from 60 minutes to more than six hours, and why a tiny island called Midway still matters to thousands of flights a year.
